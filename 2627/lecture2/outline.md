# Central Tendency: Median

- Def: If you were to order all scores of your variable from lowest to highest, then the median value is the value that splits your sample in half: half of the participants score lower than this value, and half score higher.

- Another name for the median is the 50th percentile. The nth percentile is the score that divides the sample so that % of participants score lower.

- If distribution is symmetric, median equals the mean
